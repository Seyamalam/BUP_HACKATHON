GridWise LLM

Smart Campus Energy Optimization

GridWise LLM. One API that turns plain operator notes into a valid, low cost, 24 hour energy schedule.
The problem

One campus, three power sources, 24 hours

Grid import

Electricity bought from the grid. Price changes every hour.

Rooftop solar

Free energy when the sun is up. Unused solar is lost.

Battery

Moves energy between hours. Must end the day where it started.

"Solar output will drop to about 20% from 1 PM to 3 PM."
A smart campus runs on grid power, rooftop solar, and a battery. Demand, sunlight, and price change hourly. Operators send short notes. Our job: understand the notes, respect the physics, minimize the cost.
The contract

Two endpoints

GET /health

{"status":"ok"}

POST /optimize-energy

Scenario + operator notes in, interpretation + 24 hour plan out.

The contract is two endpoints. Health returns status ok. Optimize-energy takes the scenario and notes, and returns the interpretation plus a full 24 hour plan.

The model never touches the math. Deterministic code validates, applies, and verifies everything it says.

The pipeline has six stages. Validate the request. Interpret the notes with a language model. Check the output with deterministic guardrails. Fold the directives into constraints. Solve a linear program. Then verify the plan before responding.
Interpretation

Language in, structure out

Operator note

"Solar output will drop to about 20% from 1 PM to 3 PM."

→

Directive

{ "directive_type": "solar_reduction", "hours": [13, 14], "factor": 0.2 }

✓Only the six supported directive types
✓Hours are unique, sorted, 0 to 23
✓Factors and reserves inside valid ranges
✓Invalid output re-prompts the model with the exact error
The model only handles language. A note like "solar drops to 20 percent between 1 and 3 PM" becomes a structured directive with hours and a factor. Every output passes guardrails: known types, sorted hours, valid numbers. Fail, and the model is re-prompted with the exact error.
Reliability

A hedged model chain

Gemini ×3

3.8-flash, 3.7-flash, 3.5-flash-lite fire immediately, thinking disabled.

OpenRouter ×2

Free fallbacks join 2.5 seconds later as a hedge.

First valid wins

The first response passing guardrails is used. The rest are ignored.

Repeat scenario? Cache hit — about 0.5 s, no model call at all.
Three Gemini models answer in parallel. OpenRouter follows two and a half seconds later as a hedge. The first valid response wins. Repeat scenarios skip the model entirely through a per-isolate cache.

Verification

The judge mirror

✓Energy balance holds every hour
✓Battery bounds, transitions, and rate limits
✓Solar never exceeds effective availability
✓Every directive honored in the plan
✓Final battery energy equals the start
✓Reported totals recomputed from the plan
Nothing leaves unchecked. A replay verifier mirrors the judge. It replays the plan hour by hour against every directive and every rule. The totals in the response are recomputed from the plan itself.
