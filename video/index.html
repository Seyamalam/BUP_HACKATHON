Battery

"Solar output will drop to about 20% from 1 PM to 3 PM."
The problem: a campus on grid power, rooftop solar, and a battery. Operators send notes in plain English. The service must understand the note, respect every energy rule, and minimize grid cost.

Guardrails reject

  • unknown directive types (only 6 exist)
  • unsorted / out-of-range hours
  • factor outside [0,1], reserve above capacity
  • broken note mapping or applies semantics

The LP solves, per hour

  • energy balance: grid + solar + discharge = demand + charge
  • battery dynamics, bounds, and rate limits
  • effective solar and directive windows
  • hour 23 energy = hour 0 energy
Architecture: six stages per request. Validate. Interpret. Guardrail. Fold. Solve. Then the verifier replays the plan against every rule the judge runs.
